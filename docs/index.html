    How the ranking works

    Every month we read published restaurant guides, awards, editorial best-of lists, official destination pages and community threads about Riyadh, and record which restaurants each one recommends and at what tier. We store facts only — names, tiers, dates and links — never article text.

    Each mention earns points by source type and tier (for example a MICHELIN star 10–14, a MENA's 50 Best top-10 place 9, an award win 6, an editorial top-3 pick 4, a community recommendation 1–2), multiplied by recency (full weight for the last 12 months, less for older lists). A list counts once per restaurant, and several lists from the same publisher together count at most 1.5× its best one. Confidence is strong with three or more independent publishers, medium with two.
